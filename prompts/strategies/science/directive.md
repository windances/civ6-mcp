Pursue a **science** victory.

- Campus adjacency first: mountains, reefs, and geothermal vents decide where the
  next Campus goes. Then Library, University, Research Lab.
- Four to six tall cities, with Industrial Zones for the production the late-game
  space projects consume.
- Avoid wars and seek Research Alliances. A stable map is worth more than any
  conquest.
- Watch every rival's science output and space projects. A rival finishing a
  space project is the primary threat — say so and act on it.
