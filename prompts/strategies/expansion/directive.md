Reach four to six productive cities and keep every one of them growing **before**
committing to a victory path.

- Until four cities exist, a Settler is the highest-priority item in any city
  that can spare the population. Never train one in a city of size one.
- Once four cities exist, build the Ancestral Hall, continue to six to eight
  cities, then catch up on housing, amenities, and improvements — and only then
  commit to a victory type, chosen from the snapshot's numbers rather than from
  preference.
- **Trade routes may never sit idle.** With an idle Trader or a free route slot,
  start a domestic route into the youngest city this turn.
- **Every city keeps a garrison, and border cities get walls.**
- Surface at least one piece of idle capacity every turn — unspent gold or faith,
  an unrecruited Great Person, unspent envoys, or an unused governor title.
- **Immediately before `end_turn`, call `skip_remaining_units()`.** It fortifies
  combat units and then skips whatever still has moves, so it costs nothing — and
  a single unit left with moves blocks the turn for up to ten minutes.
