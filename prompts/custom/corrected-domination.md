Pursue a **domination** victory, but finish the opening first. This is a
transition directive, not a switch to total war.

The specifics below are from turn 91. Re-derive positions, resources and turn
numbers from the current snapshot rather than assuming they are unchanged.

**Immediate (around T92-T95).**

- Mine the **Niter already inside our borders at (58,30)** and repair the pillaged
  farm at (58,29). We own Niter; do not spend a Settler founding a city for it.
- Let the Settler in the capital finish and settle the **eastern iron pocket at
  (60,31)**. We currently import our Iron on a 30-turn deal from a civilisation we
  intend to invade, and that deal dies the moment we declare war. Our own Iron is
  the precondition for Swordsmen, not an optional expansion.

**Keep. These are military infrastructure, not optional infrastructure.**

- Improvements on strategic resources (Iron, Niter, Horses) and the roads between
  them. A resource we cannot mine is a resource we do not have.
- Encampment, Barracks, and anything that shortens unit production time.
- Commercial Hub or Harbour. Unit maintenance scales with army size and our income
  is only about +20 per turn against 108 banked.
- Granary, Aqueduct, and amenities: growth and war weariness both depend on them.

**Stop.**

- Wonders, and culture buildings that do not serve the army.
- Expansion beyond the iron city.
- Any build that produces neither units, nor resources, nor the gold to pay for
  them.

**Military.**

- From Iron Working (about T95), convert the two highest-production cities to
  Swordsmen, using Magnus's Black Marketeer to economise on Iron. Keep at least one
  ranged unit per frontier city.
- Composition: melee to take cities, ranged to soften them, siege against walls.
  Never attack fortifications without a siege unit.
- Attack only at favourable odds, and heal damaged units rather than feeding them
  forward.

**Target.**

- Russia is the weakest known military (85 against our 169) and the nearest weak
  target. Egypt at 133 is stronger and is not the first fight.
- We recently paid gold and a delegation for Russian Open Borders. Declaring war
  voids that; treat it as a sunk cost, not a reason to delay.
- Five civilisations remain unmet and we have explored roughly 14% of the map.
  Scout before committing; do not march into fog on an assumption about the map.
- Keep at least one friend, so the map does not coalesce against us.

**Growth, which is a stated precondition and does not actually conflict.**

- Training units does not consume population; only Settlers do. Keep food tiles
  worked so no city stalls while it produces units.
- Get amenities positive *before* the first war. War weariness suppresses
  production exactly when the army needs it most.
