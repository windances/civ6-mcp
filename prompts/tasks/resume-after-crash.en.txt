The game exited abnormally - the process is gone, and so is the MCP. Relaunch it and
carry on from the most recent autosave.

This is not a rollback: the most recent autosave is where you already are. Loading it
costs at most half a turn, and no completed turn.

Do this in order, and do not explore:

0. Call get_game_status first and read the actual state instead of guessing it, or
   inferring it from whichever step failed. GAME STATE is one of not_running / starting /
   in_game / leader_screen / main_menu / loading / tuner_busy, and its NEXT line says
   what to do about it.
   - in_game: the game is already in a match (it reports the turn, read from the screen
     even when the tuner is not ours). Steps 1 and 3 are unnecessary; go to step 4 and
     check the turn number.
   - main_menu / leader_screen: the game is up but not in a match. Skip step 1 and start
     at step 2.
   - not_running: start at step 1.
   - tuner_busy: FireTuner accepts one connection and another process already holds it
     (get_game_status names the pid), so a second MCP can never attach. Waiting will not
     change that - stop and report. Do not launch a second game instance and do not keep
     retrying.
1. If the game is not running, launch it with launch_game and wait for it to finish
   starting (about 60-90 seconds). The menu-automation step after launch_game may
   report an error - that does not mean the launch failed, the game may be coming up.
2. Use list_saves: it is sorted by date, newest first, so entry 1 is the most recent.
   Take that one. Do not insist on the 0_MCP_ prefix - the game's own AutoSave_ files
   are often newer, and going by the prefix throws a turn away.
3. Load it with load_game_save("<that save name>"), then call get_game_overview. When
   the save asked for is the newest one, the MCP prefers the shorter route for it:
   Single Player -> Continue Game.
4. Check that the loaded turn equals the turn in the save's name (0_MCP_0077 -> turn
   77). If it does not match, do not continue - stop and report the turn you actually
   got, the save name, and the first few entries from list_saves.
5. Once it matches, re-query everything from scratch: cities, units, the map around
   them, tech and civics, policies, diplomacy, resources. Trust none of your old
   impressions.
6. Then continue, for at most 100 more turns counted from the turn confirmed in step
   4, and report when you stop.

Other rules:

- Do not load any save later than the newest autosave, and do not load an earlier one
  to recover progress.
- If the game exits abnormally again, repeat this procedure. Retry a failed load at
  most once, then stop and report - do not keep trying.
- Use the civ6-orchestrator skill and the strategy directive written into it.

When you stop, report: the turn you resumed at and the final turn, city count,
population, science/culture/gold, military strength, what you built, which wars you
fought, and every obstacle you hit (tool errors, hangs, recoveries), each with its
turn number.
