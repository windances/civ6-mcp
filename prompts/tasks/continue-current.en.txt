The game is expected to be already running with a match loaded. **Never launch it and never load a
save**: launching and loading are the human's calls, they are how a session ends up playing a
different branch (the `0_MCP_NNNN` names collide across rolled-back branches), and loading is how a
position another session is mid-turn in gets thrown away. Your first job is only to find out whether
the game can take commands. If it can, carry this match on for at most 100 more turns, then stop and
report.

0. **Capability check, read-only.** Two calls, in this order:
   - `get_game_status` - it answers with the state, the turn, and a `NEXT:` line.
   - `get_game_overview` - a real read. A status string that says `in_game` while the read fails
     means the link is not usable, and the honest answer is "cannot execute".

   Then:
   - **`in_game` and the overview returns a turn** - everything is in place. Continue with the steps
     below.
   - **anything else - STOP and report.** Do not launch, do not load, do not retry in a loop. Say
     what the state was and what it means for the human:
     * `not_running` - the game is not up; it needs a launch, and a save if no match is loaded.
     * `main_menu` / `leader_screen` - a save has to be chosen and loaded. `list_saves` is readable
       and you may report the newest few with their turns so the human can pick, but **do not load
       one yourself.**
     * `tuner_busy` - FireTuner serves exactly one connection and another process holds it (the
       report names the pid). Waiting does not help: the human either stops that process with
       `scripts\civ6-clean.ps1`, or keeps playing in that session.
     * a game on screen but FireTuner silent ("Is the game running with EnableTuner=1?") - the match
       was started outside the MCP, so no call from here can reach it. Report it; the repair is a
       restart-and-load, which is again the human's call.

   One known failure is **not** "cannot execute": the movement glitch where `get_units` reports every
   unit at 0 moves - "the turn's movement points were never granted". Re-read once; if it holds, end
   the turn anyway (the next turn starts with full moves) and do not restart the game.

1. This is a fresh session with an empty context. Call `get_diary` for the history you left yourself -
   the intent, the plans, the judgements - but take the position from the game, never from your
   notes. Then re-query it in full: `get_game_overview`, `get_units`, `get_cities`, `get_map_area`
   around every city and every unit, `get_tech_civics`, `get_policies`, `get_governors`,
   `get_diplomacy`, `get_victory_progress`, `get_trade_routes`, `get_empire_resources`.

2. **Read the temporary tasks before planning**: every `*.md` in `prompts/tasks/tmp/` (not
   `README.md`, not `done/`). Those files are instructions in force, each with its own `done when:`,
   `overrides:` and `expires:`, and nothing else in the loop knows they exist. A finished or expired
   one is retired by moving it into `done/` with the turn in its name.

3. Decide only after that, then work the turn loop: `get_game_overview`, clear whatever `end_turn`
   reports as a blocker, order every unit, set city production, then `end_turn`. **Verify state after
   a mutation that reports failure** - a refused order can still have taken effect (measured: a trade
   route that answered `NO_MOVES` while the route was established and the eureka fired).

4. Use the civ6-orchestrator skill and the strategy directive written into it (including the governor
   and Great General rules).

When you stop, report: the turn you started at and the final turn, city count, population,
science/culture/gold, military strength, what you built, which wars you fought, and every obstacle you
hit (tool errors, hangs, recoveries), each with its turn number.
