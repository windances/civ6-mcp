Loading a save is allowed in exactly one case: recovering "the current turn" after
a crash or a hang. Use the autosave written at the start of the current turn -- it
restores where you already are; it is not a rollback.

After the rollback, do not act from memory: everything you hold about cities,
units, and the map is stale. Re-query it all first -- cities, units, the map
around them, tech and civics, policies, diplomacy, resources -- before deciding
anything.
