available. The Catapult's 8 damage against it was the correct read at T109 — and the plan written
at T109 ("STOP attacking the Swordsman. Pull the field army back … pillage is the only lever")
was right. It was abandoned one turn later.

## 4. What the doctrine got right

- The assault list was the right list. The T105 roster matched "2 siege, 2 melee, 1 ram/tower,
  4 ranged" except cavalry — the composition was not the problem, its *location* was.
- The `ram-tower` goal did its job once: a Battering Ram existed at T105 because the rule demanded
  one. Its failure is that it retires on success and cannot demand a replacement.
- "No peace, ever" did not cause this. The war was not a peace-negotiation failure; the agent did
  not even know it was at war.
- The T109 reassessment (stop trading melee into a promoted Swordsman, fall back, pillage, keep the
  war formally open) was correct and specific. The failure was executing it for one turn and then
  reversing.

## 5. Proposed changes, in priority order

**A. Make readiness front-scoped (F7, F11 — highest value).**
Extend the siege-posture scan (already reports per-siege-unit `city_distance`) to also count
friendly assault units by role within N tiles of the nearest visible enemy city, and expose
`metric(assault_roles_at_front)` / `metric(assault_units_at_front)`. Then either rewrite
`siege-train` / `ranged-mass` / `melee-screen` to be front-scoped while `at_war`, or add one rule:

```
stage-the-train: when at_war >= 1 and siege_city_distance_min <= 6
                 require assault_units_at_front >= 6
```

This is the checkable form of directive.md:333–335 ("Declare only on a neighbour whose cities the
army already in place can take") — currently that sentence has no metric at all.

**B. Make the support unit a standing requirement, not a goal (F2).**
`ram-tower-before-civil-engineering` is `once: true`; the moment it is satisfied the rule is
retired for the game and the loss of the Ram is invisible. Replace with a standing rule: while at
war and before `CIVIC_CIVIL_ENGINEERING`, require `units(BATTERING_RAM, SIEGE_TOWER) >= 1` **or**
the target city has no walls. The Ram costs 65 production against ~450–500 gold in the treasury
through T109–T111 — the replacement was never a resource problem.

**C. Put the war state in the turn briefing (F8).**
`turn_start_briefing` should carry one line: `WAR: Russia (state=3, at war since T101) — their
military 76 vs ours 337; 2 enemy units within 2 tiles of the stack; last city numbers: …`. The
`get_diplomacy` flag is already read by other metrics; this costs one call and removes the class of
error that had the agent planning to declare a war it was already in.

**D. Make `melee-screen` a matchup check (F9).**
Count melee at the front by tier, and fail when the enemy has fielded higher-tier melee: with
`metric(strongest_enemy_melee_cs) >= 35`, require `units(SWORDSMAN, MAN_AT_ARMS, MUSKETMAN,
INFANTRY) >= 1` rather than any of the `WARRIOR`-class list; the message should name the tech and
gold (`upgrade_unit`) as the fix. A CS 20 Warrior is not a screen for a CS 25 Catapult when the
enemy hits at 42.

**E. Fix `mass-on-contact`'s target semantics (F11).**
Today `local_superiority >= 2` means "some enemy has two of ours within two tiles". Require it
per contacted enemy when few are in contact (`enemies_massed_on >= enemies_within_2` for
`enemies_within_2 <= 3`), or require that the enemy we actually attacked took two attacks or died.

**F. Write the defensive-war branch the directive is missing.**
The existing text assumes *we* declare (directive.md:332–335). T101–T116 is the other case: war
started while the train was 3–10 tiles out and strung along the march. The directive needs:
hold the cities, rally the stack **out of enemy range** at a named tile, replace the lost support
unit and upgrade the melee *before* advancing, do not enter the city's reach until the stack is
formed — and tie the break-off to the existing `SIEGE STALLED` signal (3 recorded turns without a
net HP drop → withdraw to the rally tile and change something before re-entering).

**G. Add rates, not just levels (F10).**
`improvements_per_10t`, `attacks_landed_per_turn`, `units_lost_recently` in the 10-turn review.
"+1 improvement per 10 turns, twice" is the kind of fact a stock rule structurally cannot see.
