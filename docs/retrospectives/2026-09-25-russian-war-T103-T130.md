## 3. What the rule file said while it was happening

`use-your-attacks` fired on eight separate turns of this war. It was **correct every time** and it
was ignored every time, because the metric behind it was dead: `build_unused_attack_query` was
missing `local y = unit:GetY()`, ran in the GameCore state where its legality test does not exist,
and treated siege units as range 1. The check existed, printed, and could not see the units. It was
fixed mid-war (T111) and the driver's own guard was added afterwards.

`carrying-capacity` fired from T111 onward and was the accurate warning of the war's real cost: the
army reached ~270 military while gold per turn sat under +10.

And the rule file had **nothing** for the failure that cost all three units — a wounded unit inside
an enemy's reach. `answer-the-attack` fired when a unit was *hit*, which is one turn too late: by
then the fix is a withdrawal, not a decision. The doctrine had the rule (`tactics/03`'s BAIT case,
computed by `formation_violations` in `play-turn.py posture`); nothing printed it where the turn was
closed.

## 4. The five laws

1. **The garrison decides the siege, not the walls or the HP pool.** 35 → 9–11 per archer shot;
   Catapults are barely affected (45–52). Moscow (garrisoned) took seven turns of fire; 圣彼得堡
   (no garrison) took five turns *including the march*; 阿斯特拉罕 (pop 3, garrisoned) took six.
2. **An ungarrisoned, wall-less city does not retaliate against melee** — 36 and 44 damage measured,
   0 taken — so melee is the cheapest damage in the stack, and the cheapest way to get there is to
   invite the sortie (T109 tripled our fire).
3. **The capture is a combat action**: the unit must MOVE (not attack), be ADJACENT at the start of
   the turn (a four-tile walk-in failed; ZOC refused the last step), and have HEALTH (a 9 HP unit
   died taking a 0/200 city). A city at 0 heals ~20 a turn, so a failed capture is a re-siege.
4. **The ring is the throughput ceiling, and LOS and movement cost are per-tile facts.** Six ring
   tiles at Moscow, three at 圣彼得堡 and three at 阿斯特拉罕; `NO_LOS` refusals at distance 2; five
   turns lost to one-tile moves that cost two movement points.
5. **A wounded unit in reach is already lost** — all three losses, same shape, 9 / 23 / 35 HP.

## 6. What the tools cost, and what they bought

Three adapter defects were found and fixed **because** of this war, all of them silent:

- `build_unused_attack_query` (missing `y`; wrong Lua state; no `Bombard`) — the `use-your-attacks`
  metric had never once been true in 19 turns of war.
- `GameState.unused_attacks` swallowed the exception by design, so the failure was invisible.
- `_click_continue_by_colour` never focused the game, so a load parked on the leader screen.

Five crashes/hangs in one session produced the recovery rules now in `AGENTS.md`: **`0_MCP_NNNN`
names collide across rolled-back branches** (use the game's per-session `AutoSave_NNNN`), and **a
turn that will not advance may be waiting for a mouse** (read the screen, try `dismiss_popup`,
before restarting).
