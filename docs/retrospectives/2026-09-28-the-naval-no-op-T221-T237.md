# The naval no-op: seven attacks that could not land, T221-T237

**A melee land unit cannot attack a unit at sea.** The manual says so in one sentence - `manual:723`,
MELEE UNITS: *"Melee units are land units which can attack enemies in adjacent land hexes. They cannot
attack enemies at sea, nor can they attack enemies more than one hex away."* This branch spent
seventeen turns not knowing it, lost two units to it, and recorded four ship kills that never happened.
Everything below is quoted from the playing session's own log and diary.

## What was lost, and to what

| turn | unit | where it died | killer | log |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T226 | 骑兵 #5177368 (CS 79) | **(74,27) - water** | 荷兰 `UNIT_CARAVEL` (CS 55) | `!!! Your 骑兵 (UNIT_CAVALRY) was killed! Last seen at (74,27).` |
| T233 | 野战加农炮 #4784149 | **(74,29) - water** (`COAST [AMBER+] {F:1 P:0 G:1 C:1}`) | the same 57 HP Caravel | T234 diary: `Field Cannon #4784149 (18/100) was killed in the AI turn by the 57 hp Caravel` |

Both were **in the water when they died** (the session's own T226 line: *"now at 6 hp on water at
(74,27) with no movement left"*), in a five-tile ferry lane its own plan had described as five water
tiles.

## The seven attacks, and what they did

Every attack this branch made on a Dutch ship, in order:

```
T222 MELEE_ATTACK|target:UNIT_CARAVEL at (75,28)|enemy HP:57 -> 57/100|your HP:100 -> 100|est damage dealt:~57
T224 ... enemy HP:57 -> 57/100 | your HP:66  -> 66
T225 ... enemy HP:57 -> 57/100 | your HP:34  -> 34
T226 ... enemy HP:57 -> 57/100 | your HP:6   -> 6
T228 ... enemy HP:57 -> 57/100 | your HP:78  -> 78
T230 ... enemy HP:57 -> 57/100 | your HP:51  -> 51
T232 ... enemy HP:57 -> 57/100 | your HP:18  -> 18
```

**Zero of seven landed damage.** The Dutch hulls' own readings say the same thing: from T221 to T237 the
values seen are `57` and `100`, every turn, with a single `94` at T223. So the diary's proud line -
*"one Cavalry has now destroyed three Caravels in four turns"*, later four - is **false**: nothing was
ever damaged, and `!!! VANISHED: 荷兰 UNIT_CARAVEL ... (killed or garrisoned?)` is the tool admitting it
could not tell ("killed **or** garrisoned?"). The crossing plan built on "the lane is clear, the Caravels
are dead" was built on that misreading.

## Why it did not evade

1. **Attacking consumes the movement; evading and attacking are mutually exclusive in a turn.** The
   Cavalry had `moves 4/4` at 34 HP and again at 6 HP - it was never pinned. It attacked first, and the
   attack spent the move.
2. **The combat estimate was wrong by 7-16x on the incoming damage.** Every attack printed
   `Est damage to attacker: ~4` (or `~2` for the Field Cannon); the AI turn then took 32, 28, 27 and 33
   HP off them in succession. At 6 HP the estimate said attacking would cost nothing.
3. **The only loud signal pushed the attack.** `CHECK FAILED [use-your-attacks]` fired on T224, T225,
   T226, T227, T228, T229, T230 and T231; `skip_remaining_units` **refused** to end the turn while a
   legal attack existed (T226: it listed `UNIT_CAVALRY@74,27 -> UNIT_CARAVEL@75,27`, and the session had
   to pass `force=True`), and T227 added `answer-the-attack` ("a unit was attacked and nothing answered
   it"). **No rule, then or now, measures "your attack did nothing" - and none asks a hurt unit to
   withdraw.** The withdrawal doctrine is prose (`tactics/03-under-attack.md`) and is not enforced.
4. **The units were placed in the water by a plan that could not see water.** The session measured it
   itself at T227: *"`get_staging_plan` DOES NOT KNOW WATER. It assigned Bombards to (73,24), (73,25)
   and (74,25) ... and labelled them `FIRE from here`"* - the water tell is the yield line (`P:0 G:1`),
   not the word `COAST`. T229 and T230 added two `NO_LOS` refusals at distance 2.
5. **The risk had been written down and not acted on.** T219's hypothesis: *"the Dutch navy ... can sink
   embarked units"*. T224's plan: *"a siege unit that meets a ship embarked is 280 production gone"*.
   T226's plan: *"Recover the wounded Cavalry ... the moment it has movement"* - it did not live to the
   moment.

## Why the manual was never opened

- **`search_knowledge` has been called zero times in this checkout**: the string appears in none of the
  56 run logs. Not in this game, not ever.
- **The tool answered first, and looked authoritative.** `get_units` printed
  `>> CAN ATTACK: UNIT_CARAVEL@74,23(100hp)`, `unit_action(attack)` printed `Combat Estimate (Melee)`
   with CS values, five promotion modifiers and a damage number. The question *"is this legal?"* never
  arose, because nothing in the loop asks it.
- **The anomaly was recorded twice and explained backwards.** The diary's own tooling lines say a
  shooter at d1 attacking a naval unit *"resolves as `MELEE_ATTACK` and still takes no damage"* - read as
  a benefit ("no retaliation"). The anomaly was that **no damage was dealt**; the tool's `est` prefix
  (`est damage dealt:~57`) was already saying it could not confirm the hit.
- **The lesson had been written down three times and lost three times.** `done/018-take-alexandria-done-T216.md`
  ("the Caravel stayed at 57/100 and the Cavalry kept all 3/3 movement"), `done/019-two-scouts-to-sea-expired-T250.md`
  and `done/021-siege-legion-overseas-expired-T270.md` (both at T225: *"the order resolved as
  `MELEE_ATTACK` and the target read unchanged"*). Each was a **task file** - the lowest rung of this
  repo's ladder - and each was retired with the branch that wrote it. Nothing in the code or the rules
  learned anything.

## What changed

1. **The Lua refuses the order** (`src/civ_mcp/lua/units.py`): when the target's `Domain` is `DOMAIN_SEA`
   and the attacker's is `DOMAIN_LAND` and the order would resolve as melee, the reply is
   `ERR:MELEE_CANNOT_ATTACK_AT_SEA|...`, citing `manual:723` and naming the way out - ranged fire at
   distance 2 (`manual:725`) or a naval unit. The guard sits **after** the ranged-at-d1 fallback, which
   is the exact path a Field Cannon adjacent to a ship used to fall through.
2. **The estimate refuses to print a number for a blow that cannot land** (`src/civ_mcp/narrate.py`):
   melee vs `DOMAIN_SEA` now reads `REFUSED BY THE RULES` with the manual citation instead of
   `Est damage to defender: ~151`. The defender's domain travels with the estimate
   (`CombatEstimate.defender_domain`, printed by both ESTIMATE lines).
3. **A counter and a staged rule** (`game_state.note_attack_result` -> `attacks_landed_nothing` ->
   `end_turn`'s metric block; the rule is `prompts/checks/pending/attacks-that-land-nothing.md`).
   On a corrected server the metric reads 0, so the rule is a **regression lock**: it fires the first
   turn the refusal is removed or bypassed. It is staged rather than live because its metric is new -
   a live rule naming an uncomputed metric reports `un-evaluable` forever (`pending/README.md`).
4. **This file.** The corrected ledger is the part a future session needs most: the Dutch navy was
   never reduced, 乌得勒支's harbour Caravel died with the city at T234, and the water north of 塞纳 is
   still theirs.

## Still open

- **`get_staging_plan` does not know water** (it will happily assign a gun to an ocean tile and call it
  `FIRE from here`) and it does not mark line of sight as unverified. Both were measured here and at
  哈勒姆. A fix should print the yield signature (`P:0 G:1`) as `WATER (embarked only)` and stop calling
  a d2 tile a firing tile before a shot has resolved.
- **No rule asks a hurt unit to withdraw.** `use-your-attacks` will keep pushing a 6 HP unit to attack
  something that cannot be damaged; a `withdraw-the-wounded` rule (metric: units under half HP adjacent
  to a stronger enemy) is the missing counterweight.
