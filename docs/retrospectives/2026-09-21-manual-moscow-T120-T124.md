# Retrospective — the hand-played Moscow turns, T120–T124

Reviewed 2026-09-21. This is the most recent battle in the game and the only one **played by
hand in the game window**, on the same save and the same game as the agent's runs. It is also
the only assault in the whole record that ended with the city changing hands.

the game's own telemetry under
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Logs`, and it is complete.

## 3. Why the human won, in four mechanisms

1. **Attacks per turn.** 5 and 7 attacks in the two decisive turns (166 damage/turn over the
   attack-turns) against the agent's 2–3 (77–83/turn). Moscow heals about 20 a turn, so the
   human's net was ~145/turn and the agent's ~60/turn. A 200-point city is one turn of work at
   the human's rate and three or four at the agent's.
2. **Order of work.** The human killed the field army *first* (T121), then hit the city (T123),
   then took it (T124) — all with ranged attacks that took no retaliation until the melee
   finishing blows. The agent bombarded first (T117–T120) and left the Russian Swordsman alive;
   it killed the Battering Ram at T127.
3. **The capture step exists.** A melee unit attacked the city centre. A support unit cannot do
   it (the agent's `CAPTURE_MOVE|…BLOCKED`), and the adapter will not attack a city tile with no
   garrison unit at all, so the one action that ends a siege was the one the agent could not
   issue.
4. **One deliberate staging turn.** T122 moved the army into range with no attacks; the agent's
   units were chronically strung out, so its attacks per turn stayed at 1–3 through the assault
   (its best turn of the whole run was four, at T108), which is why the same city took it three
   turns to break and one turn for the human to finish.

## 4. The defect this exposes

`src/civ_mcp/lua/units.py:301-332` (`build_attack_unit`) and `:502-525` (the combat-estimate
path) resolve the target exclusively through `Map.GetUnitsAt(x, y)` and bail with
`ERR:NO_ENEMY|No hostile unit at (x,y)` when the tile holds no hostile *unit*. An enemy **city
centre with no garrison** is therefore not attackable — although the game itself allows
attacking the city, as the human's T123/T124 rows show (`defender type UNKNOWN`, object 327683,
174 and 30 damage). `src/civ_mcp/lua/cities.py:171-180` (`build_city_attack`) has the same shape.

The consequence in this game was decisive: at T117–T120 the agent had ground Moscow to 0/200
and then could not touch it again; it healed to 120/200 by T126 and the assault collapsed into
a withdrawal. The telemetry now reports the city's numbers honestly, so the agent could see the
0/200 and the regrowth — what it could not do was act on it.

Proposed fix:

- In both unit-attack paths, when no hostile unit is on the tile, look for a city at the plot
  (`Cities.GetCityInPlot(x, y)`, or scan `Players[owner]:GetCities()`); if one exists and we are
  at war with its owner, proceed with a city attack. Keep `NO_ENEMY` only when the tile has
  neither a unit nor a city.
- Check `build_city_attack` for the same case: a city's ranged strike should be able to bombard
  an enemy city, not only a unit.
- Add the explicit capture step to the doctrine and the rules: when `city hp <= 0`, a **melee**
  unit adjacent to the city must move onto the tile this turn (support units can never capture),
  and a rule should fail while a 0-HP city is left standing — the agent watched one heal back
  from 0 to 120.

## 5. What the rules should take from this

- **`take-the-city`** (new): `city hp <= 0` and a melee unit within reach ⟶ fail until it moves
  in. This is the gap that cost the campaign its only broken city.
- **`use-your-attacks` should count city attacks.** Today's shortfall analysis only sees unit
  targets; the agent's T122/T128 `NO_ENEMY` probes are exactly the wasted-attack case.
- **A staging turn is legitimate.** T122 (11 moves, 0 attacks) is correct play; the rule that
  punishes unused attacks must not punish a turn whose diary line says "forming up at range 2".
- **Measure attacks against the army in range, not against the roster.** The human's edge was
  not a bigger army (16 land units vs the agent's 19 at T137) — it was that the units in range
  all attacked on the same turn.

## 6. What landed (2026-09-21)

The adapter fix and the rule that goes with it are implemented and pinned by tests
(`tests/test_city_capture.py`, 29 cases; suite 538 passing, up from 509).

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| `attack` resolves an enemy **city** at the target tile when no hostile unit is there (was `ERR:NO_ENEMY`) | `src/civ_mcp/lua/units.py` `build_attack_unit`, `build_combat_estimate_query` |
| `move` sets the ATTACK modifier and reports `CAPTURE_MOVE` for an enemy city tile, so a melee unit can walk into a 0 HP city (was a refused plain move) | `src/civ_mcp/lua/units.py` `build_move_unit` |
| a city's ranged strike can bombard an enemy city | `src/civ_mcp/lua/cities.py` `build_city_attack` |
| new capture-readiness scan: per visible enemy city, its HP pool, walls, and how many of our melee-class units are adjacent / within two tiles | `build_capture_check_query`, `CaptureReadiness` (`lua/models.py`), `GameState.capture_readiness` |
| metrics `capture_ready`, `downed_enemy_cities`, `enemy_city_hp_min` | `end_turn._capture_metrics`, `_CONTACT_METRIC_KEYS` |
| a `TAKE THE CITY` block in the turn result, naming the tile and the melee unit in reach | `end_turn._capture_event` |
| rule `take-the-city`: fails while a city at 0 HP is standing with a melee unit adjacent | `prompts/checks/turn-checks.md` (now 16 rules) |
| the melee report reads `city HP: N -> M/200` and `CITY TAKEN` for a city target | `build_attack_unit`, `_extract_pre_hp` |
| doctrine: the capture is a separate step, and an empty city is a city that can be entered | `directive.md`, `SKILL.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `tactics/06-…md`, `workers/military-map.md` |

Two cautions. A running MCP process holds the code it started with, so none of this is live until
the session is restarted — and until then the new rule reports as *un-evaluable*, because the check
engine fails a rule it cannot evaluate rather than silently skipping it. And the Lua is not
verified against the live game: there is no Lua interpreter here, so the generated chunks are
checked only for shape and block balance (`lua_blocks_balanced` in the test file, which passes over
all 108 Lua builders). The first assault after the restart is the real test.

Two bugs the live run exposed, both invisible to string tests and both now fixed and pinned:

1. the estimate's city branch concatenated `myHP`, which that function only defines after it reads
   the *defender* — an ungarrisoned city answered `operator .. is not supported for nil .. string`
   instead of an estimate;
2. the not-at-war refusal read `Cannot attack unknown`, because the city's name was resolved after
   the check that quotes it.

Still unexercised live: **the attack and the capture move themselves against an ungarrisoned enemy
city**, because the running line (T102–T103) is at peace with every civ that holds a city. The
fixture for it exists — `AutoSave_0123` is the hand-played line at T123, Moscow at `0/200` with the
converted Barbarian Horseman adjacent — so `.tools/live-capture-test.py --save AutoSave_0123
--capture` finishes the test whenever the game is free (it loads the save, orders the move and
re-scans; it never calls `end_turn`, so nothing is overwritten).
