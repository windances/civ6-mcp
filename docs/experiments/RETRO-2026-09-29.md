# Military production experiments - the cross-attempt report

The experiment is `docs/experiments/README.md`, and this file is its **result**: what each attempt
measured, which doctrine claim survived it, which was corrected, and **which artifact each correction
became**. An entry that lands nowhere is a diary note, so every correction below names its sink on the
ladder (code + test, a check rule, a task file, the directive, a tactic, or this directory).

Attempts: **A1** (`001-attempt-A1.md`, `A1-T40.json`) and **A2** (`002-attempt-A2.md`, `A2-T40.json`).

## 4. What the experiment has cost and what it has bought

- **A1:** 274 tool calls over 40 turns = **6.8 a turn**, against the ~39 a turn the previous match
  measured at T272-T287; the ten-turn reviews answered with numbers each time; five self-reports, all
  agreeing with the record.
- **A2:** 268 calls over 40 turns = **6.7 a turn**; 11 self-reports, **11 of them wrong** on the same
  column; one attack order discarded on a reply that could not be read as a result.
- **What A1 bought:** three corrections to the experiment itself (the scenario, the hypothesis, the
  stop rule), one instrument defect, and a map-geometry rule that the doctrine could not have known -
  all of them about *how the question is asked*, which is the only thing a first attempt can honestly
  settle when the map has already answered it.
- **What A2 bought:** four more corrections, and they are of a different kind - the **window** (H1 is
  unaskable inside 40 turns whatever the map says), the **recon slot** the doctrine never names, the
  instrument's **role map** (blind to the Heavy Chariot, found by the executor disagreeing with it),
  and the **combat reply** that reads like a failure when it is a hit. Two of the four were found
  *because* two attempts disagree: one compare of the same numbers twice is what makes a metric's
  defect visible at all.

## 5. Still open

- **The stop rule's metric** - the code-and-test change whose second measurement A2 supplied (76 stops
  in A1 against 11 in A2, seven in ten of them `(moves exhausted)`). Still owed, because editing
  `turn-checks.md` while A2's second half is playing would stop the rule firing for the turns that
  attempt is producing - the attempt ends at T80 or on the first city kept, and the fix lands the
  moment it does.
- **The doctrine's recon slot** - owed the same way: `tactics/01`'s establishment table has no recon
  role, so a session can satisfy it while building no scout (A2 did, and `tactics/07`'s Gate 0 stayed
  shut for a target it had met). Editing the doctrine mid-attempt is what would break the attempt's
  "same doctrine" premise.
- **The combat reply's HP label** - **landed** (`6c46a5d`), and the gold-floor detail now carries both
  measures (`0 red turn(s) by the rule ...; the diary's own gold/turn is below 10 on 40 of 40 turns`).
- **H1 has been tested and held** (T48, above) and the **siege half is on the board** (T53 and T55). What is still open is the *capture*: the march is 5-6 turns from the rally point, the target's walls/HP/garrison have still never been read by any tool (the only reads near it are `get_map_area` terrain around T41-T42), and the attempt's own finish line is *a city kept, or T80*. Q3 is therefore the live question, and the second half's one-session record (`pale-pearl-aqueduct-92`) is what answers it. Q1 has also narrowed to a specific shortfall: at T55 the table is short `ram 0/1` (unfillable under H5, above) and `ranged 3/4` (one Slinger lost in the barbarian fighting) - so "complete by T60" turns on whether the ranged slot is rebuilt, not on the siege train.
- The plan variations (A3-A6 in the protocol: Encampment timing, chops, buying the train, two war
  cities) - they only become worth running once H1 has been measured, because every one of them is a
  claim about the *ordering*.
