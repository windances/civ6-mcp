Fix the last three findings the live war produced

Three separate defects, all measured on the running branch at T95-T97.

**The frame is not settled when the turn number moves** (`end_turn.py`). The turn
result's events are a snapshot diff, and the snapshot was taken the moment the turn
advanced - which is not the moment the board is readable. Measured: the first unit
read after `end_turn` answered with the *previous* turn's values (a Warrior at 42, a
Spearman at 70, a Skirmisher at 72 where this turn's own event lines had just said
52 / 80 / 20), a Scout read one tile behind where it stood, and a move issued from
that read "walked" to the tile it already occupied while spending its movement
points. `unit_signature` (id, position, HP, movement - the three fields every stale
read got wrong at once) is now read twice, 1.5s apart, up to four times, and the
snapshot is only taken once two consecutive fingerprints agree. If they never agree
the result says so and names the read to repeat; a failed read changes nothing about
the turn. Tests: `tests/test_turn_settle.py`.

**A point-blank shot was estimated as a melee attack** (`lua/units.py`). The estimate
query's `isRanged` test was `(...) and dist > 1`, which mirrors the *fallback* branch
in `build_attack_unit` (melee after a refused ranged attempt) instead of its primary
test, `dist <= attRange`. So both live shots at range 1 printed `Combat Estimate
(Melee)` with `Est damage to attacker: ~44` and `~302` and a `WARNING: attacker
likely dies!` - while both resolved as `RANGE_ATTACK` and took no damage at all
(manual:725: ranged units always use ranged combat, even when adjacent). The
predicate now matches the attack path's. Tests: `tests/test_siege_is_ranged.py`.

**The city-strike claim in `AGENTS.md` was too generous** - it is a documentation
defect, not a code one. `city_action(city_id, "attack", ...)` on a city without walls
answers `ERR:NO_WALLS`, which is correct; a captured city-state with a Harbour and no
Ancient Walls could not hit a Barbarian Galley one tile away in its own harbour. The
reference now states the gate first and keeps the measured 43-damage figure for a
walled city, with the Chinese backup updated in step.
